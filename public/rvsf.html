What is RVSF?

Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facility

A Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facility (RVSF) is a specialized setup authorized by the government to safely dismantle and scrap old, unfit vehicles. As India moves towards greener policies, the demand for legal vehicle scrapping is booming.

Setting up an RVSF requires multiple approvals, environmental clearances, and complex documentation. That's where we come in. We are specialists in navigating the entire licensing process for you.
